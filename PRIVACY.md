# OnlySale – Privacy Policy

**OnlySale does not collect, store, or send any personal data.**

- It runs only on the supported shop websites (see the list in the README) and only reads the products shown on the page you are looking at.
- It makes **no network requests** and has no servers, analytics, ads or trackers.
- The discount range you pick is kept in the page's `sessionStorage` (per tab) and disappears when you close the panel or the tab.
- Your light/dark choice is saved in the browser's local extension storage (the only reason for the `storage` permission). It never leaves your device.

The code is open source, so you can check all of this yourself: https://github.com/RihaLabs/OnlySale
